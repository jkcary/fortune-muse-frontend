# Star Lantern Garden and story chapters

The home page and navigation link to `/garden`, a standalone twelve-level light-path puzzle. `/companion` offers quiet reflection and a small-action rehearsal; a reading can open it with its cast ID, which is checked through the existing reading access endpoint. These additions do not require a new reading, alter recorded coins, change paid content, or enable AI memory.

## Playable loop

Rotate connected path tiles to carry light from the gold source to the pearl through every required star. The first six puzzles use 4×4 boards; the next six use 5×5 boards. Later puzzles include fixed paths and three-way splits with side stars. Mutual ports and grid boundaries determine connectivity. A pearl reached without every star is not a win. The game has no countdown, lives, purchases, or random reward pool. Free guiding turns move the board toward a guaranteed solution; turns and hints are shown without a leaderboard or claimed optimal score.

After solving, explicitly keep the light. This unlocks the next level. Milestones at 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 12 levels unlock six original vector garden pieces, which can be displayed or removed independently. A finite win animation respects reduced motion. The original procedural chime defaults off, has its own device preference, and stops on mute, backgrounding or leaving. `scripts/make-garden-sound.py` reproduces the WAV without third-party samples.

Guests keep game progress on their device. Account gardens are separate; guest progress is not silently imported when signing in. Signed-in completions and decorations are stored on the server and loaded on another device. A single unfinished board (including an older replay level), moves and hints is cached separately on the current device for each account/guest. Unfinished boards do not sync across devices. Storage/load failures are visible; the client never claims a completion was saved after a failed save. A damaged guest save offers an explicit reset with confirmation. Reset affects only guest game progress, decorations and the unfinished game.

## Story and companion features

- Three chapter milestones are derived from recorded data: an explored or self-authored possibility, a chosen action, and an explicit update. Waiting, changed circumstances and pauses count as reflection; a successful life outcome is not required. After all three, users can open a chapter keepsake without exposing their question or action in the card. Following the story can continue.
- The chosen door has a personal priorities/tradeoff exercise generated by the configured AI from the actual question and context, with explicit saving and a second choice-specific AI reflection. Successful content is cached; failures offer retry without a fixed template. See `PERSONAL_OPTIONS_AND_ARCHIVE.md`. Existing hexagrams remain unchanged.
- Rehearsal can load the owner's actual small action. The user writes an observation and a request, then edits a clearly labelled sentence template. No message is sent, no AI call is made, and the rehearsal words are not persisted.
- A quiet moment records a chosen category (`joy`, `discovery`, `question`) once per local day. Joy/discovery drafts remain local and are cleared after successful saving. Open questions explicitly send their thought/fact/concern to AI for a scientific key-point reflection, capped at 200 displayed characters; question inputs and analysis stay on the current page and are not saved in garden records. See `QUESTION_REFLECTION.md`. The count is cumulative, not a streak; absence removes nothing. No notification or reminder is scheduled.
- New copy supports US English, UK English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese. Personal text and saved readings retain their own languages.

## Backend and deployment

Apply `014_star_garden.sql` after migrations 012 and 013, before starting the new backend. It adds account-owned `garden_progress` and `garden_moments`; both cascade on account deletion and are included in account export. Door exercise choices live in the existing `journey_json` and need no extra column. No payment or coin-generation changes are required.

| Endpoint | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /garden` | Authenticated user's completions, decorations, cumulative moment count and today's category |
| `POST /garden/complete` | Bounded level/rotation payload; verifies a solved canonical board and sequential unlock; locks the progress row and deduplicates retries |
| `PATCH /garden/decor` | Accepts only earned known pieces; stores an account-owned display selection |
| `POST /garden/moment` | Accepts category only; unique user/local-date constraint makes retries idempotent |

`X-Timezone` follows the existing auth/time utilities. The canonical engine is `backend/src/modules/garden/engine.ts`. `npm run contracts:sync` copies it and the reading contract into the frontend; `npm run contracts:check` checks both. Do not edit the generated frontend engine directly.

## Validation and limits

The backend suite has 126 passing tests, including all twelve puzzles' initial/solvable states, side-star requirements, fixed tiles, row boundaries, chapter milestones, ownership, locked levels, concurrent completions, earned decorations, category-only daily writes, account export/deletion, and bounded door-exercise persistence with an unchanged hexagram.

Browser acceptance scripts and screenshots live in ignored `output/playwright/garden-*` files. They exercise every level and decoration, fresh/replayed board restoration, four languages, 320px layout and 44px tile targets, reduced motion, saved door choices, chapter keepsakes, rehearsal, private draft handling, actual browser chime playback, damaged-save reset, storage-quota failures, and retrying lost completion/moment responses. Browser account/story checks use isolated synthetic route fixtures; API tests use the separately guarded `_test` database. No production user records are created for QA. The complete twelve-level guest pass and the focused `garden-replay.cjs`, `garden-edge.cjs`, and `garden-story.cjs` checks cover the final implementation.

Web/iOS/Android resource exports verify packaging. Signed installation, physical device audio/storage behaviour, and retention impact remain unverified. This first release uses single-colour light, one twelve-level chapter, fixed-position decoration slots, and editorial companion prompts. More levels, coloured-light mechanics, freely positioned furniture, social sharing of the garden and live AI rehearsal are future expansions.
